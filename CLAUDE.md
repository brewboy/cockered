# Pokémon Red: Rooster Edition – Breed Mapping

A draft mapping of all 151 original Pokémon to real chicken breeds. Evolution lines generally move from bantam or chick forms to larger or showier breeds. Types stay as in the original game.

In-game names (in **bold**) are max 10 characters (`NAME_LENGTH - 1`) and live in `data/pokemon/names.asm`, which is ordered by internal index, not Pokédex number. Names mostly blend the original Pokémon name with a chicken word.

## Notes

- Several lines reuse a breed across chick, cockerel and rooster stages so the evolution reads visually.
- Breeds like Onagadori, Phoenix and Yokohama have very long tails, which suits the legendary birds.
- Swap any mapping freely; this is a starting point for sprite work.

## Sprites

- Source art lives in `gfx/rooster_src/`: `gameboy/` (56×56, four shades, used by the game), `colour/` (56×56 RGBA, reference), `colour_x4/` (the colour set at 224×224) and `index.csv`. Files are named `NNN_<breed>.png` in Pokédex order.
- Import with `python3 tools/import_rooster_sprites.py` (defaults to `gfx/rooster_src/gameboy`), then `make`. Re-run whenever the art changes.
- The Mewtwo goose is drawn by `tools/draw_rooster_goose.py`, which writes matching gameboy, colour and colour_x4 versions.
- The Mew chick (#151) front is drawn by `tools/draw_rooster_mew.py`: the hand-drawn Serama chick body (stored in the script) lifted off the ground, with little flared wings, tucked toes, Mew's long curling tail and a few sparkles. Mew's pics live in the "bank1" section, not the Pics banks, so check that section's free space after changing them. Its back uses the `mew` option in `tools/draw_rooster_backs.py`.
- Fronts are cropped to the smallest bottom-centred 40/48/56 square. Back sprites come from `gfx/rooster_src/back/gameboy/` when a drawn one exists; otherwise they are derived from the fronts (mirrored to face right, shrunk to 32×32) as placeholders.
- Drawn backs are rear views made by `tools/draw_rooster_backs.py` (`SPECIES` holds each breed's colours, stage and options; the comment above it lists them). It writes 32×32 gameboy, colour and colour_x4 versions under `gfx/rooster_src/back/`; run it before the importer. Every species except Farfetch'd has a drawn back.
- Changing the back generator must not change existing backs by accident: regenerate into a scratch directory and compare against `gfx/rooster_src/back/` before writing into the repo.
- Farfetch'd (#83) is skipped and keeps its original sprites. Mewtwo (#150) uses `150_lab_bred_goose.png`.
- Each pics bank (`gfx/pics.asm`, "Pics 1"–"Pics 5") must stay within its ROM bank; check free space in `pokered.map` after big art changes.

## Overworld sprites and party icons

- `python3 tools/draw_rooster_overworld.py` draws the creature sprites seen on maps and writes them into `gfx/sprites/` and `gfx/icons/`; pass a folder to get a preview sheet instead. Run `make` afterwards. The art is hand-drawn pixel grids in the script.
- Shared sprites, redrawn in place: `monster` (standard rooster; also the Substitute decoy), `bird` (flapping bantam), `fairy` (Silkie), `seel` (hen floating on water; also the player's surfing sprite), `snorlax` (SNOROOSTER asleep) and `fossil` (fossil egg).
- Two new sprites reuse unused slots, so no other sprite IDs move: `SPRITE_LEGEND` (long-tailed rooster for Articuno, Zapdos and Moltres; was `SPRITE_UNUSED_GUARD`) and `SPRITE_GOOSE` (MEWHONK in Cerulean Cave; was `SPRITE_UNUSED_OLD_AMBER`).
- On sprites, `l` shows as white, `d` as light grey and `.` is see-through, so white birds must fill their bodies with `l` (the script's `fill_white` does this).
- Party icons: the `bug`, `plant`, `snake` and `quadruped` icons are now a chick, a crested Polish, a naked-neck and a tall game bird. Each species' icon in `data/pokemon/menu_icons.asm` follows its breed: chicks get the chick, eggs the fossil egg, distinctive breeds their shape, flyers the bantam, grown Water types the floating hen, and everything else the rooster. The Poké Ball icon is unused because it shares the item-ball sprite.

## Colour

- The ROM is GBC-compatible (`rgbfix -c`) and still runs on DMG and SGB. On a GBC, `engine/gfx/cgb_palettes.asm` turns the SGB palette commands into GBC palettes and BG attribute maps, and VBlank rebuilds the colours whenever `rBGP`/`rOBP0`/`rOBP1` change, so fades and flashes work unchanged. The palette engine lives in its own "Palettes" section (bank $2C).
- Each species except Farfetch'd has its own palette (`PAL_ROOSTER + n`), generated from the colour fronts by `python3 tools/make_rooster_palettes.py`, which writes `data/sgb/rooster_palettes.asm` and `data/pokemon/palettes.asm`. Re-run it after changing the art.

## Intro fight

- The opening fight is CROWGAR (Black Sumatra, seen from behind; stands in for Gengar) against NIDOROOST on Red or SILKYPUFF on Blue (for Nidorino/Jigglypuff). `python3 tools/draw_rooster_intro.py` draws them and writes `gfx/intro/` (file names kept from the original) plus colour references in `gfx/rooster_src/intro/`; pass a folder to preview instead. Run `make` afterwards.
- CROWGAR's three 56×56 poses are stored as unique tiles (tile 0 blank, tile 1 solid black, which the intro also uses for its black bars) with a 7×7 tilemap per pose; together they must stay within 108 tiles, which the script checks. The standing and wings-flared poses share their body, neck and head to save tiles.
- The scene's palette (`PalPacket_NidorinoIntro` in `data/sgb/sgb_packets.asm`) is the opponent's species palette.

## The Pit (Celadon)

- The last building on the Game Corner's row has a door at (43,19), reusing Celadon's unused warp 9 (it pointed at CELADON_MART_5F). It leads to `PIT_BACK_ROOM` (map $6B, LOBBY tiles), which has its own indoor group (`CELADON_CITY_2`). Code and text are in bank $2D with the island.
- Getting in: the slot machines count pulls in `wPitSlotPulls`. After 10, the bookie's runner (Game Corner, at 18,16) walks along the bottom row to the player and sets `EVENT_PIT_INVITED`; talking to him does the same. Without the invite, the doorman sends you back out; with it, he lets you in once (`EVENT_PIT_LET_IN`).
- The betting window: the card, birds, odds, stakes (¥1000/¥5000/¥10000) and payouts are in `data/events/pit_fights.asm`. Each fight is settled before it is told, and there's a 1-in-16 no-contest where the house keeps every bet, so every bet loses about a tenth on average.
- The stairs in the back room lead down to `THE_PIT` (map $6D, FACILITY tiles, its own indoor group `CELADON_CITY_3`). Three undercard fighters (BIKER, ROCKER, BLACKBELT; teams appended to their classes in `data/trainers/parties.asm`) take a ¥2000 entry each, in order, and pay ¥6000/¥7000/¥8000 purses on top of the normal prize money.
- Losing in the Pit doesn't black you out: `HandlePlayerBlackOut` and the overworld's after-battle check both exempt `THE_PIT`, like Oak's Lab. `ThePitAfterFightScript` checks whether anyone in the party is still standing to tell a win from a loss, and heals the party after a loss.
- After the third win the bookie sends for you. In his office he offers ¥50000 to lose to OLD IRON (GENTLEMAN team 6, a level-35 SHAMONIX). Taking it sets `EVENT_PIT_THREW_FIGHT` with no battle; refusing starts the fight, and winning it sets `EVENT_PIT_BEAT_OLD_IRON` (¥20000 purse, and the betting window won't take your bets). `EVENT_PIT_MAIN_EVENT_DONE` covers all three endings, for the Retirement Farm.
- Once Giovanni is beaten at the Viridian Gym (`EVENT_BEAT_VIRIDIAN_GYM_GIOVANNI`, aliased `EVENT_PIT_IS_RING_VENUE` in `data/events/pit_fights.asm`), the Pit reopens as a COCK RING venue: anyone can walk in, the runner stops recruiting, and the board, doorman, regulars and bookie have RING versions of their lines (the `pit_era_text` macro). The betting ban for beating OLD IRON lifts, and the bookie greets you as an old friend and says he sent OLD IRON to a farm out east. The wall by the door (44,19) reads "painted over" before and shows the COCK RING banner after.
- New saved bytes come out of the spare `ds` at the end of `wGameProgressFlags`; an assert keeps that block at 200 bytes, because any change in its size stops existing saves loading.

## Fuji Farm (east of Lavender)

- MR.FUJI's farm, where birds go when nobody wants them. Lavender's east rock wall has a gap at (19,8–9), and `FUJI_FARM` joins on as a map connection. There were no spare outdoor map IDs, so the farm uses $6E, which is numbered like an indoor map. Because of that:
  - `SetPal_Overworld` gives the farm and its buildings Lavender's palette.
  - `InitMapSprites` clears `wSpriteSetID` on indoor-numbered maps, so walking back into Lavender reloads its sprite set.
- The barn (`FUJI_FARM_BARN`, $6F) and farmhouse (`FUJI_FARM_HOUSE`, $70) use HOUSE tiles. All three maps are in their own indoor group, with code and text in bank $2E. They play the Pallet Town music.
- Until `EVENT_RESCUED_MR_FUJI`, the farmhand turns you back one step in (like the Pit's doorman). Mr. Fuji mentions the farm when he gives you the flute.
- **Retiring:** the farmer retires a party bird for good, using `RemovePokemon` like the day care.
  - She won't take your last bird, or one that knows an HM.
  - The breed is marked in `wFarmRetiredFlags`, one bit per Pokédex number.
  - The bird's nickname and species wait by the gate (`wFarmNewcomerName`/`Species`).
  - Every 5 different breeds earns a gift from `FarmGifts` (`wFarmGiftsGiven` counts them).
- **Who's about:** `FarmSyncResidents` shows or hides the toggleable residents whenever you arrive. Arriving by warp sets the map-loaded flag; arriving on foot from Lavender sets `wFarmNeedsSync`, which Lavender's script sets every frame.
  - OLD IRON appears after `EVENT_PIT_MAIN_EVENT_DONE`, with one story if you beat him and another if not.
  - The BLACKBELT's PRIMEFOWL appears in the barn after `EVENT_PIT_BEAT_FIGHTER_3`.
  - 11 flock slots (4 outside, 7 in the barn) appear as the number of retired breeds passes each slot's threshold in `FarmFlockSlots`. Each names one of your retired breeds, offset by `wFarmRotation`, which turns each visit.
- **The ledger:** the board on the barn's back wall. It lists retired breeds with a new `SPECIESLISTMENU` list menu (species names, in `home/list_menu.asm`); picking one opens its Pokédex page.
- The original game's unused hidden MAX ELIXER on map $6F is kept as `UNUSED_MAP_6F` (an alias for the barn, outside its bounds), so hidden item flags don't move.
